Based on the debate topic, you are to write an argumentative report that takes a stand on the topic (for or against) and argues within this problem space why this is the better position to take. You should still present the counterarguments to your position but have strong enough motivations for why these arguments are less important than the arguments supporting your position. You may choose to switch sides of the debate if you wish.

The report should be between 1000-1500 words, contain appropriate references and research sources (aim for no less than 10) and be written in pairs. The report covers 1.5 credits and is graded 0-100.  (U: 0-49, 3; 50-69, 4: 70-89 5: 90-100)

PS! The tip is to write the report while preparing for the debate to utilize the same efforts in these two tasks.

### Author contribution section:

At the end of the essay, you need to have a section titled:  *Author Contribution* . In this section, you describe the distribution of tasks and work by the two authors who wrote the essay.

Grading

**Grading **is based on the depth of investigation of the problem space and the elegance of execution.
Note that grades are individual and that the Author Contribution is used to determine if the students have done unequal amounts of the work.

*For grade 3 (50-69):
*You present the problem space and why it is of ethical interest. You present your position on the problem space and give at least 3 arguments for this position. You also need to present at least 2 counterarguments to your position. All arguments should point to relevant scientific/historical sources and/or data/discussions made with correct references. Some language errors and grammar mistakes are allowed.

*For grade 4 (70-89), you need to fulfil all requirements for grade 3 plus: *
You should present at least 4 arguments and 3 counterarguments that should be discussed in some depth and taken from predominantly scientific sources. There should be a convincing "red thread" between the arguments leading up to your position on the problem space. Very few language errors are allowed.

*For grade 5 (90-100), you need to fulfil all requirements for grade 4 plus: *
You should present at least 5 arguments and 4 counterarguments that should be discussed in convincing depth with scientific studies as the primary source of reference. Your argumentation should flow naturally (red thread) and it should be clear how you reach your position based on your argumentation. Next to no language and grammar errors are allowed.
*(Note that writing 5 arguments and 4 counterarguments does not automatically give you a 5. It is about the quality of the delivery.)*
