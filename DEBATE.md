
# Debate

Topic 10: Should university students be allowed to use LLMs in their examination assignments?

* **Due** **No due date**
* **Points** **100**
* **Available** **after 16 Jan 2024 at 0:00**

**Due to the large number of students, the debate may be split into two dates.**

In groups of four, you will be asked to find and construct the arguments for and against a series of ethical issues in AI (one topic per group that will be assigned by us teachers). Two students will argue "for", and two students will argue "against" said topic.

The purpose of the debate is **not **to have one of the student groups win the debate; it is to put up a convincing show to the other students that gives deep and detailed introductions to the ethical issues within the problem and the possible arguments for each side. Groups in which one side "dominates" the debate/talking time will receive lower grades. Thus, make sure that the debate, the arguments, and the talking time of the group members are appropriately balanced.

Prepare 3-5 arguments *for *and 3-5 arguments  *against * (all arguments should point to research and/or other reliable sources as to why the argument is worth considering) - it is part of the task to find these sources. We** strongly recommend** that the "for" and "against" sides work together as a team when preparing the arguments.

After the presentation, you will be asked to fill out a "group evaluation" document in which you define what you have done in the group as well as what your fellow group members have done (in your view). We use this to, to the best of our capacity, ensure that all students participate and each of you gets a fair grading for your work.

**Grading **is based on the depth of investigation of the problem space and the elegance of execution.
Note that grades are individual and different group members can receive different grades in the same group so make sure that you balance the debate appropriately.

**NOTE: The debate is strictly limited to 15 minutes per group! **
